ABOUT THIS DATA

Includes current shortages and those expected to begin within two weeks. A listing does not tell you whether a medicine is available at your pharmacy.

What is a shortage?

The company has told Fimea it cannot deliver this package for a while. Your pharmacy may still have some, or can offer a similar medicine. Ask your pharmacy. This site is not medical advice.
