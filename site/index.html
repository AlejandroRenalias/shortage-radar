    About this site

    Where the data comes from

    The list comes from Fimea, the Finnish Medicines Agency. Fimea publishes it as open data and updates it daily. This site reads that file once a day. Daily archive since 21 Nov 2026, with earlier captures from the Internet Archive.

    What one record is

    One record is one medicine package. The company that holds the marketing authorisation reported it to Fimea. A medicine can have several packages, so the counts are records, not medicines.

    A substitution group is part of Fimea’s list of interchangeable medicines. Your pharmacy decides with you and your prescriber; a group does not mean every pack size or form is suitable for you. Fimea’s interchangeable medicines.

    What the data cannot tell you

    A record that leaves the list is not proof that the medicine is back in supply. The history before this site started saving the file comes from the Internet Archive and has gaps. Dates in the history are capture dates, not exact event dates.

    Licence and how to cite

    Fimea’s data is licensed under CC BY 4.0. This site selects, joins and transforms it.
